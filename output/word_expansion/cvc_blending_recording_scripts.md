# CVC and Blending recording scripts

Two separate recordings per word: say the whole word once for the word recording, then record the complete praise line separately. Read the spelling using letter names, followed by the whole word.

File naming: `word-dad.mp3` and `praise-dad.mp3`; use the same pattern for every word. Keep CVC and Blending recordings in separate folders or ZIP files.

**Total: 48 words, 48 word recordings, 48 praise recordings (96 audio files).**
